The Planet Open

Office Mini Golf Tournament

Inspired by one of Planet's largest employee hobby groups, I wanted to create a more accessible and timeless experience that the entire office could enjoy—regardless of skill level or whether they played golf outside of work. I brought the golf course into the office with a nine-hole mini golf tournament designed to encourage friendly competition and cross-team engagement. I planned the course layout, hand-crafted a unique theme for each hole, designed all event branding and graphics, and produced supporting materials including custom scorecards, a Masters-inspired snack station, and a championship prize presentation. The activation transformed everyday office spaces into an interactive experience that celebrated employee interests while creating meaningful opportunities for connection.
