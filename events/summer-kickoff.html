Lemonade Stand

Summer Kickoff Activation

To celebrate the start of summer, I transformed a common space into a lemonade stand-inspired gathering area that encouraged employees to step away from their desks and connect throughout the day. I designed the activation concept and visual branding, curated a printed menu and shaded café-style setup, and partnered with SF Food Mart to provide fresh pastries and refreshments. The activation created a welcoming third space that fostered casual conversations, cross-functional connection, and community in the office.
