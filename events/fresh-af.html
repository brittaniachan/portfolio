Fresh AF

Planet Farmers' Market

I transformed a shared office space into a community-driven farmers' market that celebrated the hobbies and talents of Planet employees beyond the workplace. The activation featured fresh produce, jams, honey, flowers, and seedlings contributed by employee farmers and gardeners, alongside a self-guided wine tasting and juice bar to encourage employees to slow down, explore, and connect. I coordinated the market layout, vendor experiences, and overall event logistics to create an engaging environment that fostered conversation, highlighted employee passions, and strengthened workplace community.
