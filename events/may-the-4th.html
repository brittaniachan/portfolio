May the 4th

Star Wars Day Activation

To celebrate Star Wars Day, I partnered with Teaspoon to create a custom-themed boba experience that served as an afternoon employee pick-me-up. I collaborated with the vendor to develop an exclusive drink menu—including Tatooine Thai Tea, Scarif Sunset Refresher, and Skywalker Strawberry—and designed themed signage and office branding to bring the activation to life. Paired with a screening of a Star Wars film, the event transformed a simple beverage break into an engaging, shared experience that encouraged employees to gather, recharge, and connect.
