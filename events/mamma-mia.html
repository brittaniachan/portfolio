Mamma Mia!

A Cinematic Lunch Experience

I created a themed lunchtime experience inspired by Mamma Mia! (2008), transforming a standard lunch into an immersive Mediterranean escape. I partnered with long-time catering vendor SF Food Mart to curate mezze boards, charcuterie, fresh fruit, and themed refreshments, while designing custom menu cards, film synopsis signage, and cohesive event graphics inspired by the Greek islands. These low-lift activations provided employees with an opportunity to pause, share a meal, and connect over a familiar film, reinforcing workplace community through thoughtful experiential programming.
